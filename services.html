How We Help Animals Find a Better Future

Our programs give community members several practical ways to support rescue animals while they wait for permanent homes.

Adoption Process

  1. Review animals currently available for adoption.
  2. Share your household information and adoption goals with our team.
  3. Meet the animal and discuss whether the match is a good fit.
  4. Complete the adoption process and prepare your home for your new companion.

Foster Program

Foster families provide temporary homes, structure, socialization, and care while a rescued animal prepares for adoption. Foster placements can be especially helpful for animals that need a quieter environment or extra attention.

  • Provide a safe and supportive temporary home.
  • Share observations about behavior, routines, and personality.
  • Help the rescue team understand what type of permanent home may be a strong match.

Volunteer Roles

Animal Care Support

Assist with enrichment, basic care routines, and keeping animal spaces welcoming.

Transport and Events

Help animals get where they need to go and support community adoption or outreach events.

Community Outreach

Help share adoption information, volunteer opportunities, and responsible pet ownership resources.

Every Role Matters

There is no single way to help. Our team welcomes people with different schedules, experiences, and interests.
