About Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is a community-focused nonprofit dedicated to improving outcomes for animals through rescue, foster care, adoption support, and volunteer action.

Our Story

Our organization grew from neighbors who wanted practical, compassionate solutions for animals needing temporary care and permanent homes. Today, volunteers, foster families, adopters, and community partners continue that work together.

Our Core Values

Compassion
We treat animals and people with patience, dignity, and care.
Trust
We communicate honestly and work to build dependable relationships with the community.
Action
We focus on practical steps that improve the lives of animals every day.
Community
We believe lasting change comes from people working together.

Our Community Partners

We appreciate local veterinarians, foster families, adoption advocates, businesses, donors, and volunteers who help create better outcomes for rescued animals.

Partner acknowledgments are shared with gratitude because animal rescue depends on a strong and connected community.

Meet the People Behind the Mission

Volunteers bring their time, skills, and care to the work. Their contributions range from animal enrichment and transport to outreach and administrative support.
